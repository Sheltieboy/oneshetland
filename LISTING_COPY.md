## Full description  (Apple max 4000 · Google max 4000 — same copy works for both)

```
OneShetland is wir island, all in one place — a warm digital home for everyone in Shetland, and Shetlanders living away.

Whether you're after what's on this weekend, a hand finding work, a parcel brought across the isles, or just a daunder through the dialect and local history, it's all here — made for folk who live here.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• What's On — discover events, festivals and gatherings across the isles, and book tickets in seconds.
• Local & Directory — find and support Shetland businesses; see offers, opening hours and online booking.
• Fetch — island delivery: send and receive parcels, shopping and takeaways, with live updates.
• Bookings — book local services and appointments, from a haircut to a tasting.
• Jobs & Shifts — browse local jobs and short shifts, and apply right from your phone.
• Community Hubs — join clubs, charities and groups; read notices, support campaigns and hold your membership card.
• Wallet — pay local with your OneShetland wallet and earn cashback at participating businesses.
• Spik — explore the Shetland dialect, word by word, with a word o' da day.
• Memories — pin and share island stories, photos and voices on a community map.
• Da Boats — explore Shetland's fishing fleet and vessel history.
• Games — daily Shetland word and map games.

BUILT FOR SHETLAND

OneShetland is independent and made locally. Everything in one app, with community at its heart — discover, support and connect across aa the isles.

A few things to know:
• You'll need a free account to use the app.
• Some features use your location (only while you're using the app).
• Payments are handled securely by Stripe.
• You can report or block content, and delete your account at any time from Settings.

Questions or feedback? We'd love to hear from you at support@oneshetland.com.
```

  field rejects them, swap for plain `-` and `'`.

  swap to plain English if you'd rather play it straight for a wider audience.
